What is Runeboard?

A virtual tabletop built for D&D 5e — no downloads, no setup. Share a code and play.

  • 🎲
    Dice Roller Roll any dice combination with advantage, disadvantage, and modifiers. Results broadcast live to the whole party.
  • ⚔️
    Initiative Tracker DMs manage turn order in real time. Add combatants, track HP, and advance turns — all players see it instantly.
  • 💬
    Live Party Chat In-room messaging with automatic dice integration. Roll results post directly to the party feed.
  • 🗺️
    Battle Maps DMs upload maps and campaign art for the whole table to explore. Coming in Phase 2.
  • 📜
    Character Sheets & Spells Build characters and manage spell cards tied to your account. Coming in Phase 2.
  • 📋
    Session Planner DMs plan encounters, write notes, and organize campaigns before the session starts. Coming in Phase 2.
